06

Do / Don't

Do
  • Flat saturated fills, 3px borders, solid shadows
  • Keep text #111 on any color fill
  • Tilt a little, straighten on hover
  • Lift on hover (+3px), press on click
  • Animate transform and opacity
  • Use logical CSS properties for RTL
Don't
  • Gradients, blur, glow, glass, drop-shadow filters
  • Rounded-everything or soft shadows
  • More than the six palette colors
  • Theme-flipping text on a color fill
  • Letter-spacing or uppercase on Arabic
  • Animate layout properties
